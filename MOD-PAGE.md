## Full description

### The original adventure, played from inside it

HP1 VR V31 adapts Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone / Sorcerer's Stone
for Windows PC VR. Walk the familiar corridors, aim spells with your hand, draw
the lesson runes and fly a broom using the steering style that suits you.
The original game's story, objectives and puzzle outcomes remain the foundation.

### Choose how much of the adventure is physical

Hold the wand with grip and cast through the original contextual spell system.
Manipulate eligible Wingardium objects along your wand's pointing direction.
Hold the flute at your face and use trigger to play. Optional potion modes add
grip selection and, in the most physical mode, a required final drinking pose.
Pick up grounded wizard crackers and choose a trigger-driven native throw arc
or a physical swing-and-release throw.

Flight offers Stick, Point, Broom and Lean steering, adjustable sensitivity and
a synchronized broom/steering-pivot height. Broom mode supports two-hand control
and one-hand continuation. Eligible catching uses free-hand reach with a native
action fallback; the original game's catch conditions still apply.

### Make VR fit you

Choose smooth or snap turning, optional comfort masking and Cinematic Body,
Free Movement or Free Movement with a guide dot. Cinematic cameras are a
separate preference. Enable the visible Harry avatar if desired and adjust body
scale, virtual eye height, hip alignment and head-forward placement separately.

Health and bean counters can appear only when relevant, with successive events
extending their visible time. Reposition HUD elements, use wrist anchors and
scale the supported counters. Dialogue offers an optional body-relative plane;
Hermione reminders use a left-hand page. The VR menu pauses gameplay.

### Enhanced rendering, with honest limits

V31 includes stereo/procedural-effect compatibility work and adjustable world
sharpness. The separately supplied DX11 renderer and Akven ESRGAN v3 pack provide
the supported enhanced-texture path. This is not a claim that every asset is
native 4K, nor a guarantee of a particular frame rate on every headset.

### Installation

Read README.md for the exact supported download links. Extract the mod kit,
renderer and texture pack, install the Microsoft prerequisites, then run
Install.cmd. Select the original game, extracted dependencies and a new empty
destination. Verify inputs and install. Setup copies content into an isolated
installation without modifying the original game. Launch with
Launch-HP1-VR-V31.exe while SteamVR is ready.

CONTROLS.md explains every gameplay input. SETTINGS.md documents the options,
and TROUBLESHOOTING.md covers setup errors, crashes, performance and bug reports.
New installations contain no personal saves or someone else's calibration.

### Compatibility and status

Windows 10/11 x64 and two tracked controllers are required. Included bindings
cover Touch, Index/Knuckles and Vive; this is not certification of every device.
Quest headsets need a PC VR connection. Setup checks the supported original
engine/script files by hash. Arbitrary regional builds or other gameplay mods
are not automatically compatible.

The October 7, 2026 V31 snapshot passed its 58 CPU regression checks and offline
release installation checks. Full scene/device coverage is not claimed. The
latest Hermione note text adjustment still awaits in-headset confirmation.

This unofficial fan project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original
rights holders. The mod kit does not include the base game, renderer or texture
pack. Their ownership and license terms remain separate. The-Logeen's own V31
work is MIT licensed; see LICENSE, CREDITS.md and LICENSE-STATUS.md.
